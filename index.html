lore

FNAF 1

Freddy Fazbear's Pizza is a notorious family restaurant plagued by the dark legacy of the Missing Children Incident, where five children were murdered and hidden inside the animatronics, causing them to become possessed and haunted. As a night guard, you must survive five nights against these roaming animatronics, which are believed to be behind the infamous Bite of '87 that forced the location to stop daytime operations.

FNAF 2

Welcome back to the new and improved Freddy Fazbear's Pizza! Set in 1987, this grand reopening introduces high-tech Toy Animatronics equipped with advanced facial recognition tied to a criminal database. However, the dark past cannot be erased. The original, damaged animatronics rot in the back room, yet both old and new robots begin to malfunction, aggressively hunting the night guard.Through cryptic retro death minigames, the horrifying truth is exposed: the Purple Guy struck again, murdering more children. Their spirits are guided by The Puppet, a mysterious entity driving the possession of the animatronics. The chaos culminates in the infamous Bite of '87, where an animatronic bites off a daytime guard's frontal lobe. This tragedy forces the immediate shutdown of the building, paving the way for the smaller budget location seen in the first game.

FNAF 3

Thirty years after Freddy Fazbear's Pizza closed its doors, the horrific events have become nothing more than a childhood rumor. Entrepreneurs cash in on the legend by opening Fazbear's Fright: The Horror Attraction, a haunted house filled with authentic relics from the old restaurants. But the horror becomes terrifyingly real when they discover a single, operational animatronic: Springtrap.Inside this rotting green rabbit suit is the corpse of William Afton (the Purple Guy). Decades prior, cornered by the vengeful ghosts of his young victims, Afton hid inside the unstable springlock suit, which malfunctioned and crushed him alive. Now reanimated, he stalks the attraction's night guard. Through hidden arcade minigames, players can untangle the past, trigger the "Happiest Day," and finally lay the children's spirits to rest before the attraction burns to the ground.

FNAF 4

The horror comes home in FNAF 4, the definitive prequel that uncovers the tragic origin of the entire franchise. Set in 1983, players control a terrified young boy—the Crying Child—who is tormented by monstrous, hyper-aggressive Nightmare Animatronics in his own bedroom. Equipped with only a flashlight and his hearing, he must survive until 6 AM while facing his deepest fears.Through the game's retro minigames, we witness the boy's agonizing final days. He is constantly bullied by his older brother (Michael Afton), culminating in the infamous Bite of '83, where the brother and his friends accidentally shove the boy's head into the jaws of Fredbear, crushing his skull. The entire gameplay is a manifestation of the dying child's comatose nightmares, serving as the catalyst for William Afton’s descent into madness and his obsession with overcoming death.

FNAF 5

Welcome to Circus Baby's Entertainment and Rental, a high-tech underground facility where the horror takes a futuristic turn. Players step into the shoes of Michael Afton, hired as a late-night technician under an alias. Unlike previous locations, these Funtime Animatronics were specifically designed by William Afton not just to entertain, but to lure and capture children.The story unravels the tragic fate of Elizabeth Afton, William’s daughter, who was accidentally killed and possessed by the main animatronic, Circus Baby. In a shocking twist, the animatronics combine their endoskeletons into a single, collective entity known as Ennard. To escape the underground facility, Ennard tricks Michael into the "Scooper" room, eviscerating his body and using his skin as a human disguise to walk free in the outside world.

FNAF 6

The thrilling conclusion to the original saga unfolds in Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria Simulator. Outwardly a colorful business management tycoon, the game hides a dark trap designed by Henry Emily (William Afton’s former business partner). Players take on the role of Michael Afton, who manages a franchise location while salvaging horrific, withered animatronics from the back alley: Scraptrap (Afton), Scrap Baby, Molten Freddy, and Lefty.The entire facility is an elaborate labyrinth with no escape, engineered to gather all possessed entities into one place. In the true ending, Henry burns the entire building to the ground, incinerating the animatronics to melt their remnant and release the trapped souls. Michael chooses to stay and perish in the flames, while William Afton is finally sent to the deepest pits of hell, ending decades of torment.
